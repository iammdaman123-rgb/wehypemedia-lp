Common questions
Questions you're probably asking
How are you different from other agencies?

Two things: we understand your business before building a strategy, and we set up CRO audits, analytics, and tracking as standard — not as billable extras. Every campaign sits on infrastructure that proves what it earned.

What's the minimum budget to start?

₹30,000/month ad spend and above (separate from management fees). We'll tell you honestly if paid ads aren't the right starting point — sometimes SEO or organic is the better move.

How quickly will I see results?

Paid ads: meaningful data within 30–60 days. SEO: 3–6 months for consistent organic growth. We set honest timelines in week one and never promise "page 1 in 30 days."

Do I own my ad accounts and data?

Yes. Everything — ad accounts, pixels, analytics, dashboards, creative files — is set up in your name. If we part ways, you keep it all. This is non-negotiable.

What if I already have an agency?

Our Growth Audit Call shows you what's working and what isn't. If you decide to switch, we handle the full transition without any downtime.

Do you work with specific industries?

We've worked across hospitality, wellness, D2C, events, real estate, and professional services. Every engagement gets a tailored strategy — never a copy-paste template.
